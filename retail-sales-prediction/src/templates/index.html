How it works

  1. Upload a CSV or Excel file.
  2. Analyze — the pipeline auto-detects the date, target and panel-key columns; you can override every mapping.
  3. Train — every candidate model is fit on a chronological training window and ranked by WAPE on a validation window.
  4. Results — the best model is refit on train+val, scored on the untouched test window, and used to generate recursive forecasts.
